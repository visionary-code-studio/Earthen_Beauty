Our Story

The heart behind every candle

Meet Nupur

Earthen Beauty started as a small dream born from a deep love for handcrafted things. What began as a hobby — pouring wax, mixing fragrances, and experimenting with natural ingredients — quickly grew into something much more meaningful.

Every candle we create tells a story. From selecting the finest waxes and essential oils to hand-pouring each piece with care, we believe that the beauty of a candle lies not just in its flame, but in the love that goes into making it.

Today, Earthen Beauty offers a wide range of handmade products — from scented candles and reed diffusers to wax sachets and home fragrances — all crafted to bring warmth, comfort, and a touch of luxury to your everyday life.
